# Homepage V2 — connected AI team

## Core product rule

**ALVO is the interface. OREM is the depth. Together, they are the team.**

- ALVO is the daily-use private business chat and the primary interface for the customer team.
- OREM is the subscription upgrade for deeper reasoning, advanced analysis and automation.
- ALVO and OREM share the same trusted business context, data layer, permissions and provenance rules.
- They are not independent products. The product experience should make collaboration and escalation feel natural.
- When a request is straightforward, ALVO handles it.
- When a request needs more depth, the system can bring OREM into the workflow according to the customer subscription and configured controls.
- OREM can perform deeper work and return the outcome into the same ALVOREM experience.
- Public demos must not pretend OREM escalation or automation is live until the backend supports it.

## Homepage UX rule

The homepage should make three things understandable within seconds:

1. ALVO is where the user starts and what they use every day.
2. OREM is the upgraded subscription layer for deeper work and automation.
3. Both agents operate as one private AI team with shared business context.

## Brand rule

Visible ALVO and OREM names in product UI use the branded `AgentWordmark` component and the approved O treatments rather than generic text wordmarks.
